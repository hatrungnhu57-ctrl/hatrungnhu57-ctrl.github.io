About the studio

Independent by choice. Curious by nature.

Self-fundedVietnam-basedEarly stage

N-DIGIHUB is a self-funded software development project based in Vietnam. Its creator, Trung Nhu Ha, has published DataDay and ClipboardVault Smart Keyboard on the Apple App Store while building new mobile and web products. This is an independent initiative, not a claim of incorporation or a large team.

Our work spans native iOS tools, web utilities, and design-led experiments. We iterate with modern development tools and AI-assisted workflows while prioritizing honest product information and continuous improvements.
